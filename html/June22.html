Monday's Man

Today was Monday, and what a Monday it was. I had to have a hard conversation with someone who reports to me. It was something I've been dreading since Wednesday of last week. I don't want to get into too many specifics, but today seems like it may be an ideal whiskey day.

Some days you go to work and you come home and you need something to take the edge of. I know that sounds like I may be an alcoholic, I dunno about that, but I plant to have a glass. My go to whiskey is this Bird Dog brand that comes in peanut butter flavor. I'm down for most things that come in a peanut butter variant! That probably also makes whiskey drinkers cringe, but I drink what I like.

I have tried other variants of whiskey and I like Wild Turkey, if I'm going to drink straight whiskey it's pretty good. I don't really care for Jack Daniels or Crown Royal. I purchased a small bottel of straight Crown recently to give it a try. It was garbage! I really did not like it. I also purchased a small, basically single shot, of a blackberry Jack Daniels, and that was pretty good. I know I just said I don't like them, but I was referring to the straight variety!

Print update on GridCube

I mentioned a few days ago about a project I'm working on in my personal life making a GridCube box. I received the files for the corner stl's and I printed one that failed and then printed a few more. I have since printed 8 total corners successfully, with no more failures.

I was also perusing the KickStarter site to get more information on when I will get all the files and found a parts list to customize the build. I definitely will need the magnets and the acrylic panels, because I want to use these to transport my miniatures from point A to point B. The rest of the files look like they will not arrive until shortly before or after July 4th. I would assume it's after the holiday weekend though.

My current system for transporting these items is via a tool called Modi-box. Which was also a kickstarter my friends and I heard of. We missed the kickstarter, but we bought the files off of MyMiniFactory and shared the cost.

It's an ok system, and I have included pictures of what it looks like below. It works ok, but due to the high point cost of our armies and the size of our models they have to be taller to accomodate them. Which is fine, but I dunno, it seems a little flimsy. The other nice thing about the GridCube though is you can use it for things beyond miniatures including cards, display cases, tool holders, etc.

In my modi-box models below you can see that I have put down a metal tape on each floor with a plate that pulls out. I then glue magnets on the bottom of my models so they adhere to the plate. This minimizes movement when I ship them around and go to games. I will still use something similar with the GridCube system, and I doubt I will throw away the cases that I have.

Father's Day. I feel like I've had a heading exactly like this a few times this week. I likely have. Today is officially Father's Day. We did not have specific plans, but I did know that for dinner I wanted to grill some steaks. So step 1 was getting some steak. I also know that I was low on charcoal so step B was getting some of that. That led to the conclusion that I needed to go to Walmart.

Ideally for steaks at the best price I would go to Sam's Club, but I really didn't want to turn a trip to the grocery store into a whole day affair, so Walmart it was. I don't normally like buying meat from that store for a variety of reasons. One, like most stores, they hide fat and poor cuts of meat in the package. Meaning what it looks like you are getting is not necessarily what you are getting. I'm also aware of a few videos online that show tha Walmart is saying meat sold there is heavier than it actually is. This means you are over paying. Even still for convenience sake I purcahsed some thick T-Bones there.

Afterward we went home for lunch and had some family reuinion left overs. Katie brought home some uneaten fried chicken, pasta salad, buffalo dip, and rice crispy treats. It wasn't glamorous but it was good.

Katie and I then watched a few episodes of Silo. I started it over with her so she could keep up with it. I figured she would like it if she gave it a shot, and since it was Father's day she went along with it. I think she really liked it though.

I then did something I was putting off. My resin printer in the basement worked a hole in the FEP for the resin tank. The FEP is the clear plastic film that at the bottom of the resin tank. It holds the liquid resin in, while allowing UV light in from the screen. It flexes and allows for the plate to remove itself from the screen without damaging it. Over time as you print, they will wear out, and this one had been going for a long while.

I have two resin tanks that way when one produces a hole I can move to the other one. The other tank didn't have a hole in it yet, but it was also pretty old. If I'm going to replace one I may as well replace the other while I'm at it. I don't really like the process though, its time consuming and the toxicity of the resin always makes me worried working with it for any amount of time.

He got me a deck of insults that I can use when playing DnD with my friends, a Mr.Potato Beholder (also from DnD) and a can of mixed nuts. I appreciate the thought. Like Katie's side of his family though he always does stuff last minute. For instance he purchased those today, the day of Father's Day. If it was up to him he would do the same with Christmas gifts.
